## Top Product Discovery Platforms Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on E-commerce Site Search, Product Discovery, Merchandising, Recommendations & AI-Powered Shopping Experiences*  

**Last updated: August 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Product Discovery**. These tools power on-site search, category browsing, autocomplete, personalization, recommendations, and merchandising to help shoppers find products faster and increase conversion rates for online retailers.



**Examples** include Constructor, Searchspring, Klevu, Nosto, Bloomreach Discovery, Algolia, Luigi’s Box, Empathy.co, Coveo, and Unbxd (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project for search engines, vector/hybrid search, e-commerce-optimized indexing, and related open tooling — ideal for developers and commerce teams seeking high-performance, self-hosted, or transparent alternatives to commercial product discovery platforms.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[Constructor](https://constructor.com/)**  

  AI-first product discovery platform focused on search, browse, recommendations, and merchandising that optimizes for revenue and conversion using behavioral signals.



- **[Searchspring](https://searchspring.com/)**  

  E-commerce search and merchandising platform designed to improve product discovery, personalization, and on-site conversion for online retailers.



- **[Klevu](https://www.klevu.com/)**  

  AI-powered site search and product discovery solution popular with mid-market and Shopify merchants, featuring semantic understanding and merchandising tools.



- **[Nosto](https://www.nosto.com/)**  

  Personalization and product discovery platform that delivers recommendations, search, and targeted experiences across the customer journey.



- **[Bloomreach Discovery](https://www.bloomreach.com/)**  

  Enterprise product discovery and search solution that combines AI, merchandising, and customer data capabilities within a broader digital experience platform.



- **[Algolia](https://www.algolia.com/)**  

  Developer-first search and discovery API known for speed, relevance, and extensive frontend integrations used widely in e-commerce and beyond.



- **[Luigi’s Box](https://www.luigisbox.com/)**  

  Product discovery and search platform focused on relevance, analytics, and merchandising for e-commerce sites.



- **[Empathy.co](https://www.empathy.co/)**  

  Search and discovery platform emphasizing privacy-friendly, relevant product experiences and open technology approaches.



- **[Coveo](https://www.coveo.com/)**  

  AI-powered relevance platform that delivers unified search and product discovery experiences across commerce, service, and website use cases.



- **[Unbxd](https://unbxd.com/)**  

  E-commerce search, merchandising, and personalization platform designed to improve product findability and conversion.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[Meilisearch](https://github.com/meilisearch/meilisearch)**  

  Lightning-fast, open-source search engine with typo tolerance, hybrid search, and an intuitive API — excellent for e-commerce product discovery experiences.



- **[Typesense](https://github.com/typesense/typesense)**  

  Fast, open-source, in-memory search engine designed as a developer-friendly alternative to Algolia, with strong support for typo tolerance, faceting, and vector search.



- **[Elasticsearch](https://github.com/elastic/elasticsearch)**  

  Powerful distributed search and analytics engine widely used for large-scale product catalogs, full-text search, and complex relevance tuning (note licensing changes in recent versions).



- **[OpenSearch](https://github.com/opensearch-project/OpenSearch)**  

  Community-driven, open-source fork of Elasticsearch that remains fully open and is frequently used for e-commerce search deployments.



- **[Apache Solr](https://github.com/apache/solr)**  

  Mature, open-source search platform built on Lucene with rich features for faceting, highlighting, and large-scale product indexing.



- **[Vespa](https://github.com/vespa-engine/vespa)**  

  Open-source big data serving engine that supports search, recommendation, and ranking at massive scale — suitable for advanced product discovery systems.



- **[Typesense + InstantSearch / Frontend Libraries](https://github.com/)**  

  Open-source UI components and connectors that make it easy to build Algolia-like instant search experiences on top of Typesense or Meilisearch.



- **[Meilisearch E-commerce Examples & Plugins](https://github.com/meilisearch)**  

  Official and community examples, Shopify/WooCommerce connectors, and ranking customization tools for product catalogs.



- **[Vector & Hybrid Search Extensions](https://github.com/)**  

  Open-source projects adding semantic/vector search capabilities on top of traditional keyword engines for modern product discovery.



- **[Merchandising & Ranking Rule Engines](https://github.com/)**  

  Community tools and frameworks for implementing business rules, boosts, and merchandising logic alongside open search engines.



- **[Catalog Indexing & Sync Pipelines](https://github.com/)**  

  Open-source connectors and ETL scripts that keep product data synchronized from e-commerce platforms into search indexes.



- **[Recommendation System Prototypes](https://github.com/)**  

  Open-source collaborative filtering, content-based, and hybrid recommendation projects that complement search-based discovery.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **Developer-friendly engines**: Meilisearch and Typesense are the most popular modern open-source choices for fast product search.

- **Enterprise scale**: Elasticsearch/OpenSearch and Vespa remain strong for very large catalogs and complex ranking needs.

- **Classic power**: Apache Solr continues to serve many high-volume e-commerce deployments.

- **Frontend & UX**: InstantSearch-style libraries and open UI components accelerate implementation.

- Many specialized **e-commerce search**, **faceting**, and **personalization** projects continue to appear on GitHub.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine **Meilisearch or Typesense** (or Elasticsearch/OpenSearch for larger scale) as the search engine, open frontend components for the search UI, product catalog sync pipelines, and optional ranking/merchandising rule layers to create a complete self-hosted product discovery solution.
